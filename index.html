How It Works

From diagnosis to daily habit

Practices Onboard & Prescribe

Practices onboard with HeartCoach and get a tool they can prescribe directly to their patients. Roughly 1 in 3 cardiac patients don't complete their recommended follow-up — HeartCoach re-engages that population and brings them back into the office.

Source: npj Digital Medicine, 2021 — only 56.7% completed 90-day cardiology follow-up after discharge (Cleveland Clinic, 39,209 patients).

🤝

Physician Trust, Built In

Prescribed by the patient's own physician at the point of care — so HeartCoach arrives with trust already built in, not as a random wellness app. One moment in the office becomes a personal coach that's with them every day.

Patients receive personalized daily coaching — medication reminders, lifestyle nudges, symptom triage, and wearable integration. 30 seconds a day, every day.

About HeartCoach

Built to solve the engagement crisis in cardiac care

75% of health app users abandon within the first week. Cardiac patients are the hardest population to activate — and the most consequential to lose.

HeartCoach was built from the ground up using five proven behavioral science frameworks: Nir Eyal's Hooked Model, BJ Fogg's Tiny Habits, loss aversion mechanics, physician trust networks, and social proof. Not guesswork — evidence.

We're a standalone technology company that licenses our AI coaching platform to cardiovascular organizations around the world. One product, every cardiovascular network.
